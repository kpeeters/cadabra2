Tutorials

The tutorials listed below are meant to illustrate Cadabra concepts at the level of simple examples; for research-level applications of Cadabra please see the papers section.

The pages linked below are produced from actual Cadabra notebooks. Instead of reading them on-line, you can also download the notebooks and play with them inside Cadabra. If you have a nice notebook that you think would help others to learn Cadabra, please get in touch.
